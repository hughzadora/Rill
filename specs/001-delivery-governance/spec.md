# Delivery governance

A project owner can describe an idea in ordinary language and decide whether to accept the resulting change at merge. The advisor captures desired outcomes and unresolved assumptions. The implementer uses the Spec Kit record to deliver and verify the change. A maintainer can reproduce verification and continue development from the repository.

Product behavior, public API, and release policy changes require a specification, plan, task list, and acceptance record. Routine maintenance requires a rationale and verification results. The owner reviews the classification and outcome. Independent enforcement requires a contributor identity and a check producer outside the contributor's control.
